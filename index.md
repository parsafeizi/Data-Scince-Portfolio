## About Me
My name is Parsa Feizi, and I am a UNC Charlotte student double majoring in Data Science and Business Analytics. I am interested in analytics, business intelligence, and using data to improve decisions and business processes. I enjoy working on projects that turn raw data into clear insights, especially when the results can help people understand a problem, compare options, or make a process more effective.

Through my coursework and projects, I have gained experience with Python, R, SQL, Excel, Tableau, and the full data project workflow, including collecting data, cleaning it, analyzing patterns, building visualizations, and communicating results. I am continuing to build both my technical skills and my ability to explain data in a way that is useful to different audiences.

My career goal is to work in an analytics role in Charlotte where I can support better decision-making, contribute to business intelligence work, and keep developing my technical and leadership skills.
